OUR ORIGIN & MISSION

Built to Outperform. Built to Last.

MK Industries was founded in Punjab with a singular guiding philosophy: heavy industrial machinery should never compromise on metallurgical strength or mechanical precision.

What began as a specialized tool-and-die and machining workshop has evolved into one of northern India's premier engineering facilities for corrugation plants, high-speed rotary die cutters, and custom factory automation. We source certified structural steel plates, machine components on high-precision CNC boring centers, and balance every drive roller dynamically to eliminate harmonics.

Heavy Metallurgy

Certified structural steels and chrome alloy fluting rollers with induction hardening.

Precision Dynamics

ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 dynamic balancing for zero machine vibration at top speed.

QUALITY PROTOCOLS

Our Rigorous Quality Framework

No machine leaves our production facility without passing four distinct quality gates.

Micro-Tolerance Metrology

High-precision laser micrometers and coordinate measuring ensure shafts and bearing housings maintain sub-0.05mm fit tolerances.

Induction Surface Hardening

Critical rollers, gearboxes, and knife blocks receive induction heat treatment reaching HRC 58-62 surface hardness.

Dynamic Balancing

All rotating drums, slitter shafts, and feed rollers are dynamically balanced up to 150% of maximum operating RPM.

72-Hour Load Testing

Completed machines undergo 72 hours of uninterrupted dry test runs with thermal imaging to monitor bearings and gear meshing.

ISO 9001:2015 Process

Comprehensive batch traceability, certified raw mill test certificates, and full documentation for every fabricated assembly.

Lifetime Spare Support

We maintain rapid-dispatch inventory of rollers, bearings, cutter blades, and pneumatic components for immediate replacement.
